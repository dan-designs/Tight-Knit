The Night

Twelve hours, open air.

Twelve hours of live music, live painted murals, an art pop-up and auction, the Sticker Bus, and open walls for anyone to paint. Park Raiser raised support for a permanent public Art Park in Richmond, a space where muralists, graffiti artists, and creatives of every skill level can work.
